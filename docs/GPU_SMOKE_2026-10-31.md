# Tang GPU experiment log — 2026-10-31

## Scope and reproducibility
Actual local GPU runs on Tang, not a hypothetical/dry-run. The original video and frames remain on Tang; do not commit private media, adapters or trained weights.
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Laptop GPU, 16 GiB
- Python: 3.10.12; PyTorch 2.10.0+cu128; Transformers 4.57.6
- Isolated environment: Python venv with system-site packages.
- Visual model: `HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM2-500M-Video-Instruct`.
- Official Hugging Face host timed out on Tang; downloaded from `https://hf-mirror.com` to a local directory.
- `Pillow>=10.4` was required; previous system Pillow lacked `Image.Resampling`.
- Weights are excluded from the repository.

## Actual inference (synthetic smoke only)

| Probe | Output | Correct? | Generate time (s) |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Red rectangle | "The large rectangle is red." | Yes | 0.449 |
| Blue rectangle | "The large rectangle is blue." | Yes | 0.129 |
| Three-frame red → green → blue | "The first color appears first and the last color appears last." | **No** | 0.357 |

All **3 inferences executed successfully**, but **only 2/3 answers were correct**.
Peak allocated memory: **2.007 GiB**.
This is a simple fixture smoke test, *not* a research benchmark or general VLM accuracy.

## Actual LoRA training (synthetic smoke only)

- PEFT adapter: rank 4, alpha 8, target modules `q_proj` and `v_proj`.
- Trainable parameters: 557,056 of 508,039,360 (0.1096%).
- Step 1 training loss: 5.18959; step 2: 4.36856.
- Peak allocated GPU memory: **6.122 GiB**.
- The adapter was actually saved to Tang at `outputs/lora_probe/adapter/`.
- **No held-out accuracy gain is claimed.** Two steps on two synthetic colors do not establish improvement.

## Qualitative local video probe

A 110.388 s existing local video was sampled at 5 relative time points. Single-frame and 5-frame prompts both ran on GPU, respectively requiring 1.34 s and 1.476 s generation. This is an unlabeled qualitative probe only. Frames, original video and generated natural-language descriptions are **not uploaded**.
